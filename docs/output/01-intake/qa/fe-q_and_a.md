## Q-FE-04 — Blink sharing UX
- Why it matters: the approval Blink must be copied to X/Discord by the agency owner in 2 clicks.
- Options: A) "Share as Blink" button on an approval row: copies `https://dial.to/?action=solana-action:<url>` and shows a preview iframe/`@dialectlabs/blinks` component. B) Plain URL copy only.
- Default (used if unanswered): A
- AI recommendation: A, confidence medium. Reality check (research 02 §1): X never unfurls Blinks natively; the public inspector is `https://www.blinks.xyz/inspector`; `@dialectlabs/blinks@0.22.5` renders a Blink in our own page.
